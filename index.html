💌 a letter from Adi
My dearest Maby,
Reading your letter made me smile — and tear up a little too, not gonna lie. Hahaha. Salamat kaayo sa tanan. For the patience, for the arrangements, for waiting up for me even when work keeps me longer than I'd like. I know it's not easy — 1.5 hours of travel, a demanding schedule, and a boyfriend who sometimes disappears into overtime. But the fact that you still show up, still make the effort, still wait — that means everything to me. I'll be honest — I get anxious too. When you want to see me and I can't be there right away, it eats at me. I hate the distance. I hate that sometimes work gets in the way of us. But I love that you understand my limits, and I love you even more for never making me feel guilty about them. And yes — I overthink sometimes. I know. Hahaha. But it only comes from loving you too much and wanting to protect what we have. So please be patient with me when those episodes come. All I need is you — your presence, your assurance, your "I love you." That's enough to calm everything. I always think about a future where we don't have to count travel hours or squeeze moments between deadlines. Where I can be close to you, always. That's what I'm working toward. That's what keeps me going. I love you so much, B. More than I probably say out loud. But I hope you feel it in every small thing I do.
💜
Always yours, Adi 💜
